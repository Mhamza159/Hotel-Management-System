<objective>
Implement Plan 07-01: Guest Loyalty, Verified Reviews, Wishlists & Automated Waitlist Notifications

1. Verified Stay Reviews (ENGAGE-02):
   - `POST /api/v1/reviews`: Submit review with 1-5 rating & comment. Strictly verified: guest must own a booking with `checked-out` or `completed` status for that room.
   - `GET /api/v1/rooms/:id/reviews`: Public paginated reviews for a room.
   - Auto-aggregate average rating and review counts on the Room model.

2. Guest Loyalty Points Engine (ENGAGE-01):
   - Accumulate points on checkout ($10 spent = 1 point earned).
   - Points balance query `GET /api/v1/loyalty/balance`.
   - Redeem points during booking checkout (100 points = $10 discount).

3. Cancellation Waitlist Dispatch (ENGAGE-03):
   - `POST /api/v1/waitlist`: Join waitlist for sold-out roomType and date range.
   - Hook into `CancellationService.approveCancellation` & `CronService.releaseExpiredBookings`: Automatically notify active waitlisted guests when inventory frees up.

4. Guest Wishlist (ENGAGE-04):
   - `POST /api/v1/wishlist/:roomId`: Add room to wishlist.
   - `DELETE /api/v1/wishlist/:roomId`: Remove room from wishlist.
   - `GET /api/v1/wishlist`: Retrieve bookmarked rooms with live pricing.

5. Integration Tests & Postman Documentation:
   - 100% automated test coverage in `tests/integration/engagement.test.js`.
   - Update Postman collection with `Guest Loyalty, Reviews & Wishlist (Phase 7)` folder.
</objective>
